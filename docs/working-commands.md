# Hear Alexa skill: working voice commands

The current development invocation name is **test development**. Replace it with the public skill name before sharing this guide with listeners.

## Open and get help

- "Alexa, open test development."
- "Help."

## Find and play content

- "Play news."
- "Play sport."
- "Play something about gardening."
- "What's trending?"
- "Play from a talking newspaper."
- "Play from Talking News Federation."
- "Play the latest publication."
- "Play a publication from Talking News Federation."
- "Play the latest from Tynedale Talking Magazine."

## Combine a date with a search

Dates can include **today**, **yesterday**, or **this week**. A date can be combined with a category or topic, organisation, creator, or publication.

- Category or topic: "Play today's news."
- Category or topic: "Play yesterday's sport."
- Category or topic: "Play today's content about gardening."
- Organisation: "Play today's content from Talking News Federation."
- Creator: "Play today's content from Tynedale Talking Magazine."
- Publication: "Play today's publication from Talking News Federation."

## Choose between similar results

When Alexa reads several matches:

- Say one of the names Alexa offered.
- Say "show more" to hear more matches.
- Say "yes" to confirm a selected match.
- Say "no" to leave the selection flow.

## Control playback

- "Pause."
- "Resume."
- "Stop."
- "Next."
- "Previous."
- "Start over."
- "Rewind 30 seconds."
- "Fast forward 30 seconds."
- "What's this about?"
- "Who is the creator?"

Hear automatically plays the next recording in the current queue. When returning later, the listener may be asked whether to continue an unfinished recording.

## Change playback speed

- "Play faster."
- "Play slower."
- "Set playback speed to 1.5."
- "Normal speed."

Supported speed options depend on the recording. Hear supports speeds from 0.5 to 2 times normal and remembers the listener's choice.

## Feedback and safety

After Hear asks for feedback, say:

- "Enjoyed."
- "It was okay."
- "Not enjoyed."
- "Skip."
- "Report this content."
- "Report this creator."

## Follow creators and receive notifications

- "Follow this creator."
- "Unfollow this creator."
- "Enable notifications."
- "Check my notifications."
- "Disable notifications."

Alexa may ask the listener to grant notification permission before alerts can be enabled.

## Finish

- "Cancel."
- "Exit."
- "Goodbye."

If Alexa misunderstands, wait for the prompt and repeat one of the shorter commands above.
